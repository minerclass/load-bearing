3D is not available on this device, so the game is running in plan view. Everything works the same way.

Two bridges can look the same from the road.

You have six lessons and twelve hours to get a class across one question: was Reconstruction a success, a failure, or both, and for whom? Each lesson builds one bay of the bridge. Then three loads cross it.

Look underneath whenever you like. Where a thin lesson sits matters, because each bay leans on its neighbours.
