Load-Bearing

Two bridges can look identical. Only the load test tells you which one was built.

The site engineer

“You have six lessons and twelve hours to get a class across the gorge of an argument: Was Reconstruction a success, a failure, or both — for whom? Every choice you make either adds structure under the deck or shine on top of it. From the road, nobody can tell the difference. The load can.”

  • Pick 1 of 3 cards per lesson slot — watch the bridge take shape.
  • Hold the X-ray (or press X) any time to see what's really under the deck.
  • Mind the budget. Deep work costs time. Shortcuts are free. That's why they tempt.
  • Then the load test: Recall, Application, Transfer — each heavier than the last.

Built on Micah Miner's pedagogical-friction framework (noetic friction; desirable difficulties; the artifact of learning vs. the learning). ← Games Hub
